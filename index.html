About

I currently pursuing graduate studies in Earth and Planetary Sciences at the SKL, MUST. Holds a bachelor’s degree in Medical Information Engineering from the School of Medical Information Engineering at AHUCM, where academic performance consistently ranked among the top students, Obtained postgraduate exemption recommendation qualification.

My research focuses on the interdisciplinary field of Mars rover, environmental and ecological remote sensing, and bioinformatics. I have previously interned as an algorithm engineer and a deep learning image algorithm intern, specializing in computer vision and stereo matching.

With a multidisciplinary research background, I specialize in data mining, model development, and the synthesis of academic findings.
